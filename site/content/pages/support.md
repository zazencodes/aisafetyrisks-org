---
title: Support this project
description: How to support AI Safety Risks.
---
AI Safety Risks is an independent educational project. The explainers are free to watch and share, with no advertising and no tracking.

We do not accept donations yet. We plan to set up a nonprofit structure for this work, and donations will open once it is ready, so that support goes to an accountable organisation.

In the meantime, the most useful things you can do are:

- **Share an explainer** with someone who would find it useful.
- **Report errors.** Accuracy is the point of this project, and careful readers make it better.
- **Suggest papers** that deserve a clear explanation.
